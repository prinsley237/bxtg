Technical Expertise with a Long-Term Vision

BXTG is a Cameroonian technical consultancy founded in 2004. It began with geotechnical design, technical studies, supervision of construction works and laboratory services, and has since grown into a multidisciplinary practice.

Engineers and architects lead the work, and key technical staff are registered with their national professional orders.

Established by the late Emeritus Engineer TOUOMOU MOFFOU Severin Blaise.
